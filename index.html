ESP

Shows on screen what the player should not see.

  • Boxes and skeleton: outline of every player, visible through walls and buildings.
  • Info: name, distance, weapon and health bar.
  • State: who is hidden, crouching or downed.
  • Items: cash, loot and vaults marked on the map.
  • Max distance: limits the range to keep the screen clean.
Na práticaToda informação que o jogo esconde de propósito fica exposta. Surpresa e furtividade deixam de existir.

Aimbot

Moves the aim automatically to the target.

  • Aim key: hold a button and the aim locks.
  • Field of view: a circle defines how far the aimbot pulls.
  • Smoothing: makes movement less robotic to look human.
  • Body point: choose head, chest or nearest.
  • Prediction: anticipates targets running or inside vehicles.
  • Filters: ignores teammates and already-downed players.
Na práticaA "suavização" existe só para enganar quem assiste. O próprio recurso admite que o objetivo é passar por habilidade real.

Radar

A minimap showing everything around you.

  • Players: position of everyone, including behind you.
  • Vehicles: cars and who is inside.
  • Loot and cash: where the valuable items are.
  • Settings: size, range and map rotation.
Na práticaQuem usa radar não precisa ouvir, observar nem se comunicar com o time. As habilidades que o jogo recompensa perdem o valor.
